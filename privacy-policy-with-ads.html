🔋 BatteryBuddy - Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 28, 2025

Overview: BatteryBuddy stores your device data locally. We use third-party advertising services that may collect certain information to provide personalized ads. We are transparent about all data practices.

1. Introduction

Welcome to BatteryBuddy! This privacy policy explains how we handle your information when you use our Android application. We take your privacy seriously and are committed to being transparent about our data practices.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

BatteryBuddy collects and stores the following information locally on your device:

  • Bluetooth Device Information: Names, MAC addresses, and battery levels of Bluetooth devices you choose to add
  • Reminder Settings: Times you set for charging reminders
  • App Preferences: Your chosen alarm sounds and notification settings
  • Usage Data: Timestamps of when you check battery levels (stored locally)

2.2 Information Collected by Ad Networks

BatteryBuddy displays advertisements provided by third-party ad networks. These networks may collect:

  • Advertising ID: A unique identifier for advertising purposes (can be reset in device settings)
  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, screen size
  • IP Address: For approximate location (city/country level)
  • Ad Interaction Data: Which ads you view or click
  • App Usage: How you interact with the app
Important: Ad networks operate independently and have their own privacy policies. We recommend reviewing their policies:
  • Google AdMob: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Other ad networks as implemented

3. How We Use Your Information

3.1 Core App Functionality

The locally stored information is used to:

  • Display battery levels of your Bluetooth devices
  • Send you charging reminder notifications at times you specify
  • Track battery history for your devices
  • Provide low battery warnings (30%, 20%, 10%)
  • Improve your user experience with the app

3.2 Advertising

Information collected by ad networks is used to:

  • Display relevant advertisements
  • Measure ad performance
  • Prevent fraud and abuse
  • Personalize ads based on your interests

4. Permissions We Request

BatteryBuddy requests the following Android permissions:

  • Bluetooth: To detect and connect to Bluetooth devices
  • Bluetooth Scan (Android 12+): To scan for nearby Bluetooth devices
  • Location (Android 10-11): Required by Android for Bluetooth scanning (we don't track your location, but ad networks may use approximate location)
  • Notifications: To send you charging reminders
  • Vibrate: To vibrate when reminders trigger
  • Schedule Exact Alarms: To ensure reminders fire at the exact time you specify
  • Wake Lock: To wake your device for reminders
  • Internet: Required for displaying advertisements

5. Data Storage and Security

5.1 Local Data (App Functionality)

  • Local Storage: All device data is stored in an encrypted Room database on your device
  • No Cloud Sync: Your device data never leaves your device
  • No Account Required: We don't collect email addresses, names, or any personal identifiers
  • Secure: Data is protected by your device's security (lock screen, encryption)

5.2 Ad Network Data

  • Ad networks collect and process data according to their own policies
  • Data is transmitted securely over HTTPS
  • You can limit ad tracking in your device settings

6. Data Sharing

6.1 Your Device Data

We do NOT share your device battery data or personal information with anyone.

6.2 Third-Party Service Providers

We work with the following third parties:

  • Advertising Networks: To display ads (they collect their own data as described above)
  • No Analytics Services: We do not use analytics services
  • No Data Brokers: We do not sell data to brokers

7. Advertising

7.1 How Ads Work

BatteryBuddy displays advertisements to support development and keep the app free. These ads:

  • Are provided by third-party ad networks
  • May be personalized based on your interests
  • Collect limited data as described in section 2.2
  • Can be limited through device settings

7.2 Your Choices

You can control ad personalization:

  • Android Settings: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization
  • Reset Advertising ID: Settings → Google → Ads → Reset advertising ID
  • Premium Version: Consider upgrading to remove ads (if available)

8. Children's Privacy

BatteryBuddy is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

Important for Parents: Ad networks may collect limited data from all users. We recommend reviewing ad network privacy policies and using parental controls on devices used by children.

9. Your Rights and Choices

You have control over your data:

  • Access: You can view all your device data within the app
  • Modify: You can edit or delete devices anytime
  • Delete: Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all local data
  • Opt-Out of Personalized Ads: Use device settings to limit ad tracking
  • Reset Advertising ID: Reset your ad ID in device settings

10. Data Retention

  • App Data: Retained only while the app is installed. Deleted upon uninstallation.
  • Ad Network Data: Retained according to ad network policies (typically 90-180 days)

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted here with an updated "Last Updated" date. We recommend checking this page periodically. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

12. GDPR Compliance (EU Users)

If you are located in the European Union, you have additional rights under GDPR:

  • Right to Access: You can access all your data in the app
  • Right to Erasure: Uninstall the app to delete all local data
  • Right to Portability: Data is stored locally and accessible to you
  • Right to Object: You can opt out of personalized advertising
  • Legal Basis: We process data based on your consent (by using the app) and legitimate interests (advertising)

Data Controller: For device data: You (stored locally). For advertising data: The respective ad networks.

13. California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

California residents have specific privacy rights:

  • Right to Know: This policy explains what data we and our partners collect
  • Right to Delete: Uninstall the app to delete local data; contact ad networks for their data
  • Right to Opt-Out: Use "Do Not Sell My Personal Information" settings for ad networks
  • No Discrimination: We don't discriminate based on privacy choices

14. Technical Details

For the technically curious:

  • Database: Room Database (SQLite) - Local only
  • Encryption: Android's built-in encryption
  • API Level: Minimum Android 7.0 (API 24)
  • Network Usage: Used only for displaying advertisements
  • Background Services: WorkManager for periodic battery checks (local only, no data transmission)
  • Ad SDKs: Third-party advertising SDKs integrated (as implemented)

15. Data Transfers

Device Data: Never leaves your device.
Advertising Data: May be transferred to ad network servers (typically in the US and EU). Ad networks use standard contractual clauses and other safeguards for international transfers.
