🔋 Battery Buddy - Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 29, 2024

TL;DR: Battery Buddy respects your privacy. We don't collect personal data, everything stays on your device, and we only use anonymous analytics to improve the app.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Battery Buddy! This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data when you use our app. We believe in transparency, so we've made this as clear as possible.

Developer: Battery Buddy
Contact: edward.silverx@gmail.com

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Data We DO Collect

  • Device Battery Data: Battery levels, device names, and connection status of your Bluetooth devices (stored locally on your device only)
  • App Usage Analytics: Anonymous data about how you use the app (via Google Analytics/Firebase):
    • Which features you use
    • How often you open the app
    • Crash reports to fix bugs
    • General device info (Android version, device model - no personal identifiers)
  • Ad Interaction Data: Google AdMob collects anonymous data to show relevant ads (see section 4)

2.2 Data We DON'T Collect

  • ❌ Your name, email, or phone number
  • ❌ Your location
  • ❌ Your contacts
  • ❌ Your photos or files
  • ❌ Your Bluetooth pairing keys or passwords
  • ❌ Any personally identifiable information

3. How We Use Your Data

All battery data (device names, battery levels, history) is stored only on your device. We use it to:

  • Display battery levels and predictions
  • Send you reminders (if you enable them)
  • Show battery history charts
  • Update the home screen widget

Anonymous analytics data helps us:

  • Fix bugs and crashes
  • Understand which features are most popular
  • Improve the app experience
  • Decide what features to add next

4. Third-Party Services

Battery Buddy uses the following third-party services:

4.1 Google AdMob (Advertising)

We show ads to keep the app free. Google AdMob may collect:

  • Device advertising ID (you can reset this in your phone settings)
  • Ad interaction data (clicks, impressions)
  • General device info (OS version, device model)

AdMob Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Opt-Out: You can opt out of personalized ads in your Android settings: Settings > Google > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization

4.2 Google Firebase Analytics (Optional)

We use Firebase for anonymous crash reports and usage statistics.

Firebase Privacy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

5. Data Storage & Security

  • Local Storage: All your device data (battery levels, history, settings) is stored locally on your device in an encrypted database
  • No Cloud Sync: We don't upload your data to our servers
  • No Account Required: You don't need to create an account or log in
  • Data Deletion: Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all your data

6. Permissions We Request

Battery Buddy requests these Android permissions:

  • BLUETOOTH & BLUETOOTH_ADMIN: To detect and read battery levels from Bluetooth devices
  • BLUETOOTH_CONNECT: (Android 12+) To access connected Bluetooth devices
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: (Android 13+) To send you charging reminders
  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM: To schedule precise reminder notifications
  • INTERNET: To show ads and send anonymous analytics
  • AD_ID: To show personalized ads (you can opt out in Android settings)

We do NOT request: Location, Camera, Microphone, Contacts, Storage, or any other invasive permissions.

7. Children's Privacy

Battery Buddy is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you are a parent and believe your child has used this app, please contact us and we'll help you understand what data (if any) was collected.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. Significant changes will be announced in the app.

9. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA Compliance)

If you're in the EU or California, you have these rights:

  • Right to Access: Request what data we have (spoiler: almost nothing!)
  • Right to Deletion: Request deletion of your data (just uninstall the app)
  • Right to Opt-Out: Disable analytics and personalized ads in settings
  • Right to Portability: Export your data (contact us for help)

10. Data Retention

  • Device Battery Data: Stored locally until you delete the app or clear app data
  • Analytics Data: Automatically deleted by Google after 14 months (configurable)
  • Ad Data: Managed by Google AdMob according to their retention policy

11. International Data Transfers

Anonymous analytics and ad data may be processed by Google servers in the United States and other countries. This data is anonymized and complies with GDPR and privacy regulations.

12. Do Not Track

Battery Buddy respects "Do Not Track" signals. However, third-party ad networks (Google AdMob) may not. You can opt out of personalized ads in your Android device settings.
